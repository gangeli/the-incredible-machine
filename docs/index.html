Three ways to play

The browser version is a web app: put it on the home screen and it opens full screen with its own icon, works offline and keeps your progress, exactly like a store app. That is how to play on an iPad or iPhone, where nothing else can be installed.

iPad & iPhone

  1. Open the game in Safari (not Chrome).
  2. Tap Share, the square with an arrow.
  3. Scroll down, tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Android tablet & phone

  1. Open the game in Chrome.
  2. Tap the purple Install button on the title screen, or the browser's ⋮ menu → Install app.
  3. Prefer a real APK? Download it and see the sideloading steps.

Computer

  1. Open the game in Chrome or Edge (Firefox and Safari play it in a tab).
  2. Click Install on the title screen, or the small install icon at the right end of the address bar.
  3. It shows up in your apps and launcher like any other program.

The home-screen copy updates itself the next time it opens with a connection. The web version and the APK keep separate progress.

What's inside

44 puzzles

From "put the ramp under the ball" to chain reactions with switches, wires, motors, belts, buckets and pulleys. Every goal fits in one sentence.

Free play

Every part, no goal, category tabs in the tray. Build the machine you want and press Play.

Ropes, belts & wires

Tap the tool, then the two things to join. Ropes run over pulleys, candles burn them, scissors cut them.

Mort & Pokey

A mouse who scurries after cheese and a cat who pounces when he sees him. Cage the cat, feed the mouse.

Made for small hands

Big parts that snap into place, forgiving drops, a giant Play button, a hint bulb, and a nudge on the first puzzles.

Tiny and private

How to play

  1. Pick a puzzle. The goal is written at the top in one sentence, for example Get the ball into the bucket.
  2. Drag parts from the tray on the right onto the playfield. Parts snap into place; a green frame means the spot is free, a red frame means it is taken.
  3. Tap a placed part to flip it, turn it, or throw it back in the tray.
  4. To tie a rope (or a belt or a wire), tap the rope in the tray, then tap the two things to join. For a rope you can tap pulleys in between. Tap a rope to cut it again.
  5. Press the big green Play button and watch. Press the red Stop button to try something else.
  6. Stuck? The light-bulb button shows where the parts should go for a few seconds.

Installing the Android APK

The APK is the same game as a native Android app. It is not on the Play Store because this is a hobby project, so Android asks for permission once.

  1. On the tablet, open this page in Chrome and tap Android APK.
  2. When the download finishes, tap the notification (or open the Files app and tap app-release.apk).
  3. Android says it needs permission to install apps from this source. Tap Settings, switch on Allow from this source, then go back.
  4. Tap Install. If Play Protect asks, choose Install anyway.
  5. Find Incredible Machine in the app drawer and play. Updates install over the old version and keep your progress.

You can switch the permission off again afterwards. The APK is built and signed by the GitHub Actions workflow in the repository, so what you download is exactly what the source code produces.

About

An open-source fan remake of Sierra/Dynamix's 1993 puzzle game. The parts, physics and puzzles follow the original's rules (balls, ramps, seesaws, trampolines, conveyors, fans, balloons, candles, cannons, rockets, dynamite, Mort the mouse and Pokey the cat) with new vector art, big touch targets and a one-sentence goal per puzzle, so that a six-year-old can play without reading much.

One Kotlin code base runs everywhere: the physics, parts and screens compile for Android and for the browser, and the pictures on this page are exported from the game's own renderer.
