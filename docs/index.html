TheIncredibleMachine

A tablet remake of the classic contraption puzzle game, built for small hands.

Play in your browser

Works on iPad, iPhone, Android and computers. Nothing to install: it runs right here, and it keeps working offline once loaded. Progress is saved in the browser.

Or download the Android APK

Android 7.0 or newer. The whole game is about 100 KB: no ads, no internet, no accounts.

Put it on the home screen (iPad, iPhone, Android, computer)

The browser version is a web app: add it to the home screen and it opens full-screen with its own icon, just like an app from the store, and it works without a connection. This is the way to play on an iPad or iPhone, where nothing can be sideloaded.

  1. iPad or iPhone: open the game in Safari (it must be Safari, not Chrome). Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow), scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add. Launch it from the new icon.
  2. Android tablet or phone: open the game in Chrome. Tap the ⋮ menu and choose Install app (older versions say Add to Home screen). Chrome may also offer an Install banner on its own.
  3. Windows, Mac or Chromebook: open the game in Chrome or Edge and click the small install icon at the right end of the address bar (or ⋮ → Cast, save and share → Install page as app).

The home-screen copy updates itself the next time it is opened with a connection. The web version and the APK keep separate progress.

How to install the APK on an Android tablet

  1. On the tablet, open this page in Chrome and tap Download the APK.
  2. When the download finishes, tap the notification (or open the Files app and tap app-release.apk).
  3. Android will say it needs permission to install apps from this source. Tap Settings, switch on Allow from this source, then go back.
  4. Tap Install. If Play Protect asks, choose Install anyway: the app is not on the store because it is a hobby project, not because it does anything unusual.
  5. Find Incredible Machine in the app drawer and play! Updates install over the old version and keep your progress.

You can switch the "allow from this source" permission off again afterwards. The APK is built and signed by the GitHub Actions workflow in the repository, so what you download is exactly what the source code produces.

How to play

  1. Pick a puzzle. The goal is written at the top in one sentence, for example Get the ball into the bucket.
  2. Drag parts from the tray on the right onto the playfield. Parts snap into place; a green frame means the spot is free, a red frame means it is taken.
  3. Tap a placed part to flip it, turn it, or throw it back in the tray.
  4. To tie a rope (or a belt or a wire), tap the rope in the tray, then tap the two things to join. For a rope you can tap pulleys in between. Tap a rope to cut it again.
  5. Press the big green Play button and watch. Press the red Stop button to try something else.
  6. Stuck? The light-bulb button shows where the parts should go for a few seconds.
  7. Free play gives you every part with no goal at all; the tabs at the top of the tray sort them into balls, building blocks, machines, animals, power, goals and ropes.

About

This is an open-source fan remake of Sierra/Dynamix's 1993 puzzle game. The parts, physics and puzzles follow the original's rules (balls, ramps, seesaws, trampolines, conveyors, fans, balloons, candles, cannons, rockets, dynamite, Mort the mouse and Pokey the cat) with new vector art, big touch targets and a one-sentence goal per puzzle so that a six-year-old can play without reading much.
