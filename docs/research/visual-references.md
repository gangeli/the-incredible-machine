# The Incredible Machine - visual reference index

Reference screenshots for the 1993 DOS game *The Incredible Machine* (TIM1, Sierra/Dynamix,
Jeff Tunnell Productions), its expansion *The Even More Incredible Machine* (TEMIM, 1993, same engine),
*The Incredible Machine 2* (TIM2, 1994, DOS 256-colour), *The Incredible Machine 3* (TIM3, 1995, Windows
re-release of TIM2) and the spiritual successor *Contraption Maker* (Spotkin, 2014).

All files were verified with `file` to be real images and are larger than 5 KB. Every image was viewed
before being described. Sources that were blocked (MobyGames -> Cloudflare 403, Fandom -> 403/402,
classicdosgames.com -> bad TLS cert, dosgames.com/gamesnostalgia -> 403) were replaced by DOS Games
Archive, LaunchBox GamesDB, Internet Archive, VGMPF, Wikipedia, The Spriters Resource and Steam.

Note on file types: DOS Games Archive serves its "*.gif" screenshots as 640x480 JPEGs; those are saved
here with a `.jpg` extension. The TIM2 captures from that site are 640x441 (the bottom ~39 px of the
native 640x480 screen is cropped in those captures).

## 1. TIM1 - The Incredible Machine (DOS, 1993, 640x480, 16 colours)

### tim1_title.png (640x480, 4-bit PNG, 18 KB)
Source: LaunchBox GamesDB "Screenshot - Game Title" (https://images.launchbox-app.com/66368675-acda-4a5d-bba9-563699c0e33b.png)
The title screen. A solid teal (#00a0a0) background. In the centre sits a bevelled steel plate
(rounded corners, four rivets) carrying the logo: the word "INCREDIBLE" in red serif capitals on a white
strip, and "MACHINE" in huge chromed 3-D block letters whose "I" is a screw and whose "A" contains a
steel nut/gear. Above the plate is "THE" in red and a small conveyor belt driving three gears; the
copyright line "(c)1992 Sierra On-Line, Inc." sits under the plate. Scattered around the edges are
animated part sprites in their in-game look: an orange girder/incline (thin diagonal gold "ladder"
segments), a blue bowling ball, a goldfish bowl, a windmill, an electric fan, a wall socket, a white
cat on a girder, a mouse, a red balloon on a string, scissors, a cannon, a pulley with rope, a
magnifying glass, a see-saw, a red boxing glove on a spring, and a baseball on a long girder. This shows
the whole TIM1 part art style: 1-2 px black outlines, flat 16-colour fills, no anti-aliasing.

## Layout measurements (measured on the native 640x480 captures with PIL scanlines)

### TIM1 / TEMIM play screen (640x480, 16-colour VGA)
- Whole screen: 640x480.
- Playfield interior (teal #00a0a0): x = 8 .. 567, y = 8 .. 359 -> **560 x 352 px**, top-left at (8,8).
  It is surrounded by a bevelled frame: a brown (#905c3c) bar with a yellow (#f0f000) highlight edge,
  ~8 px thick on the top/left/right and ~12 px on the bottom (frame bottom ends at y ~ 372).
- Parts bin column: x = 568 .. 639 (**72 px** incl. frame); bin interior (cyan #00f0f0) x = 576 .. 631
  (**56 px** wide), y ~ 10 .. 359. The bin's own right frame is orange/gold (#f0b000).
  From the top of the bin: a small picture button (the puzzle picture / go-to-intro-page) y ~ 9-58,
  then a gold button with left/right arrows (scroll bin) y ~ 68-88, then the part slots from y ~ 98 down.
  Each slot is an icon of roughly 32-40 px with a small count digit centred beneath; slots repeat about
  every **48-50 px** (e.g. icons at y ~ 125, 180, 225 with counts at ~145, 205, 245).
- Score strip: y = 373 .. 479 (**~107 px** tall), black background. It holds three groups: "SCORE"
  (6 digit wheels), "BONUS 1" (4 wheels) and "BONUS 2" (4 wheels). Labels are red serif capitals on a
  wood-grain banner; wheels are white digits on black with wood framing and small gold rivets.
- Grid: no visible grid in any screenshot; parts sit at arbitrary pixel positions (level files store
  pixel x,y). Long parts are built from repeated tiles (girder/incline pieces, conveyor belt segments,
  brick-wall segments about 8 px tall), which is the only regularity visible.

### TIM1 / TEMIM puzzle-select (intro) page
- Same frame, bin and score strip as above; the playfield is replaced by a cream "book" panel inset
  ~24 px from the playfield edge (x ~ 32 .. 543, y ~ 36 .. 340).
- Gold scroll banner with the puzzle title: x ~ 69 .. 494, y ~ 40 .. 55, text in white 8-px-ish serif
  capitals, format "PUZZLE n: TITLE".
- Left card ("Control Panel"): x ~ 40 .. 255, y ~ 65 .. 335: icon row (play, gravity/air arrows,
  speaker, bomb, stop; wrench, picture), then "Gravity" scroll label + slider strip (x ~ 66 .. 228,
  y ~ 200 .. 235), then "Air Pressure" label + slider strip (y ~ 275 .. 310); slider thumb is a gold knob
  on a ruled track under the strip.
- Preview window: x ~ 272 .. 527, y ~ 72 .. 236 (a ~255 x 165 miniature of the playfield, teal).
- Goal text box: x ~ 272 .. 527, y ~ 255 .. 333, yellow (#f0f000) rounded rectangle, black serif text,
  left-aligned, wrapped to ~40 characters per line.

## Colour palette style
- TIM1/TEMIM use a fixed 16-colour VGA palette (4-bit). The palette extracted from the LaunchBox PNG:
  0 #000000 black, 1 #905c3c brown, 2 #a00000 dark red, 3 #f0b000 orange/gold, 4 #f0f000 yellow,
  5 #fcfcfc white, 6 #00a0a0 teal (playfield), 7 #00f0f0 cyan (parts bin), 8 #f00000 red,
  9 #00a000 green, 10 #0000f0 blue, 11 #c0c0c0 light grey, 12 #707070 dark grey, 13 #00f000 bright
  green, 14 #0000a0 dark blue, 15 #ffffff white. Sprites have 1-px black outlines, flat fills, and
  dithering is rare; steel parts are light/dark grey, girders orange with black/yellow rungs, ropes
  are 1-px gold lines, belts 1-px black lines.
- TIM2/TIM3 use 256-colour VGA: saturated cartoon palette, sky-blue (#7090f0) default backdrop,
  gold/orange bevelled UI frames with light-cyan panels, purple/green tiled menu backgrounds, white
  goal box, red/yellow logo.
- Contraption Maker: full-colour HD cartoon art with anti-aliasing and soft gradients.

## How the goal text / puzzle title are presented
- TIM1/TEMIM: the puzzle title ("PUZZLE 1: TUTORIAL: PUT THE BALL IN THE HOOP") appears ONLY on the
  intro/puzzle-select page, in white capitals on a gold scroll banner; the goal sentence appears there
  in a yellow rounded box in black serif text. During play the playfield shows no title or goal; the
  player returns to the intro page via the picture button at the top of the parts bin.
- TIM2: the goal sentence is always visible in the white rounded box under the playfield, in large
